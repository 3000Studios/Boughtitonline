# Profitability gate

A working checkout is not evidence of profitability. New setup spending in this run is $0; existing Shopify/platform costs remain real business expenses.

The verified bundle price is $49. WELCOME10 reduces first-order item revenue to $44.10. Taxes are not margin. Obtain actual processor fees, digital-delivery costs, refunds/chargebacks, licensing/royalty obligations and monthly fixed costs before projecting net profit.

For each product:

`contribution = collected item revenue - payment fees - supplier cost - shipping - delivery/app cost - expected refund/chargeback cost - acquisition cost`

For the store:

`monthly profit = sum(order contributions) - monthly fixed expenses`

`break-even orders = ceiling(monthly fixed expenses / positive average order contribution)`

Use actual Shopify Payments rates for the owner's plan, actual supplier quotes for the destination and variant, and actual sales data. Do not assume zero acquisition cost merely because traffic is organic: record any production or promotion expense.

Before importing a physical product, document supplier rating, exact variant, warehouse stock, landed cost, real delivery window, fulfillment funding, returns handling and clean images. Price 2.5–3 times verified landed cost, at least $9.99 with .99 endings, then recalculate after WELCOME10 and fees. Reject products with negative or unverified contribution. No gadgets are currently approved by this gate.

Prioritize the existing instrumental bundle for organic audience promotion while physical fulfillment is unresolved. Do not buy ads, paid plans, samples or fulfillment in this $0 build. Real profitability requires positive net profit from real orders over a defined reporting period; no such result has been verified.
